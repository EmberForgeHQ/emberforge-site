Meet Ember Relay.

A coordination tool for the people behind the community. Relay helps staff share availability, find useful overlap, submit schedules, and keep recurring reminders moving without turning coordination into a second administrative job.

Why Relay exists

Coordination should not live in somebody’s head.

Community teams are often volunteers with different schedules, time zones, and real lives. Relay is being shaped around a simple goal: make it easier to answer “when can everyone actually be here?” and keep the routine follow-up from falling on one person every week.

01 / AVAILABILITY

Share when you’re actually free.

Team members can set and review their own availability without digging through chat history or rebuilding the same schedule every week.

02 / OVERLAP

Find the useful windows.

Relay compares submitted availability so teams can spot shared time instead of manually playing calendar Tetris.

03 / SUBMISSIONS

Know who has checked in.

Weekly availability submissions give coordinators a clearer picture of what is complete and what still needs a nudge.

04 / REMINDERS

Let the routine chase itself.

Recurring reminders can handle the “please submit your availability” loop so one staff member does not have to become the human notification system.

Built from something real. Ember Relay is evolving from Phoenix Coordinator, a tool originally built to solve coordination problems inside a live community rather than from an imaginary feature checklist.
Current direction

Small tool. Useful job. Room to grow.

Relay is still early. The current direction stays deliberately focused on staff availability and coordination first, then expands only where the workflow proves it needs more machinery.

FOUNDATION

Availability profiles

Set, view, and maintain personal availability in a predictable format.

CORE

Overlap and submissions

Compare team windows and track whether weekly availability has been submitted.

NEXT

Recurring coordination

Use scheduled reminders and clearer completion status to reduce manual follow-up.

LATER

Only what earns its place

Future additions will follow real coordination needs rather than turning Relay into an everything-app wearing a calendar hat.
