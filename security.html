Security

Found something that should not be possible?

Please report security issues privately and give us enough detail to reproduce and understand the problem. Do not post vulnerabilities, credentials, private data, or exploit steps in public Discord channels.

REPORT PRIVATELY

Email the details.

Use ever_forge@outlook.com with a clear subject such as “Security report: Ember Vault.” Include affected product, impact, reproduction steps, and evidence that helps us verify the issue.

PLEASE AVOID

Do not make the problem larger.

Avoid accessing data that is not yours, disrupting services, deleting or altering data, social engineering, denial-of-service testing, or publishing the issue before we have had a reasonable chance to investigate.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

We will treat serious reports seriously.

We will review the report, ask for clarification when needed, work toward a fix, and communicate when we can. Security communication stays calm and specific. This is not the place for theatrical forge metaphors.

EmberForge is still early. This page is responsible-disclosure guidance, not a bug bounty program or a promise of payment.
