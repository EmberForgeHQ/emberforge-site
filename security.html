Security

Found something we should know about?

We take responsible security reports seriously. If you discover a vulnerability affecting EmberForge, Ember Vault, Ember Nexus, or another EmberForge service, please report it privately so we can investigate before details are shared publicly.

Security reports should stay private. Please do not post credentials, tokens, private account information, exploit details, or vulnerability reports in public Discord channels.

How to contact us

Email ever_forge@outlook.com with enough detail for us to reproduce or understand the issue.

What to include

Tell us what you found, which EmberForge product or service is affected, the steps needed to reproduce it, and the potential impact if you can reasonably determine it. Screenshots, logs, request IDs, or other non-sensitive evidence can help.

Please avoid

Do not access data that does not belong to you, retain private data, disrupt services, perform destructive testing, social-engineer users or staff, or publicly disclose an unresolved issue before we have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate.

What happens next

We'll review the report, confirm receipt when practical, investigate, and work toward a fix based on severity and impact. We may ask for additional reproduction details as we investigate. Serious security situations get calm, useful communication. No theatrics.
