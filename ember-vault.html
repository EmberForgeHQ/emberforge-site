A configurable Discord economy platform for communities that want wallets, marketplaces, rewards, vouchers, role income, and administration without rebuilding the entire machine themselves.

Built for real communities

Useful before impressive.

Ember Vault is built around the jobs community teams actually have to do: configure an economy, delegate management safely, reward participation, run shops, and keep each Discord server's data isolated.

01 / ISOLATION

Guild-isolated.

Each Discord server keeps its own wallets, transactions, settings, shops, vouchers, and rewards.

02 / CONFIGURATION

Shape it around your server.

Choose your currency, starting balance, manager role, log channel, and economy behavior.

03 / ACCESS

Delegate without over-permissioning.

Give trusted managers economy access without handing out full Discord Administrator permissions.

04 / MARKETPLACE

More than a wallet.

Build shops, inventories, vouchers, rewards, and redemption flows around the way your community works.

05 / AUTOMATION

Reduce repetitive work.

Role income, scheduled rewards, and future automation features are designed to take routine admin work off your plate.

06 / GROWTH

Start simple. Leave room.

Begin with a useful economy now and grow into analytics, advanced controls, and richer administration later.

EmberForge DNA

The same Forge. A calmer tool.

Ember Vault inherits EmberForge's dark material surfaces, crafted geometry, turquoise and purple accents, and human language. But because it handles economies, permissions, transactions, and configuration, the product itself stays more restrained.

Reliable first. Clear states, predictable actions, readable information.
Flexible where it matters. Communities should be able to shape the economy around themselves.
Personality in moderation. A competent treasurer who occasionally makes a joke.
