BUILT FOR REAL COMMUNITIES

Useful before impressive.

Ember Vault is built around the jobs community teams actually have to do: configure an economy, delegate management safely, reward participation, run shops, and keep each Discord server's data isolated.

Guild-isolated

Each Discord server keeps its own wallets, transactions, settings, shops, vouchers, and rewards.

Configurable

Choose your own currency, starting balance, manager role, log channel, and economy behavior.

Delegated safely

Give trusted managers economy access without handing out full Discord Administrator permissions.

Marketplace ready

Build shops, inventories, vouchers, rewards, and redemption flows around the way your community works.

Automation friendly

Role income, scheduled rewards, and future automation features reduce repetitive admin work.

Built to grow

Start with a useful economy now and grow into analytics, advanced controls, and richer administration later.

EMBERFORGE DNA

The same Forge.
A calmer tool.

EmberForge owns the design system: Forge Cut geometry, dark material surfaces, controlled cyan, clear hierarchy, and human language.

Ember Vault inherits those bones, then shifts toward a quieter teal-cyan palette, nested framing, and more operational layouts. It should feel like the finished instrument sitting on the workbench.
